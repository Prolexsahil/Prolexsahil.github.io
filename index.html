About

      
I'm a senior postgraduate at IIT Gandhinagar in the Lingo Research Group, advised by Prof. Mayank Singh. My thesis is IndicTalk, a conversational system for Indian languages, built on a dataset of more than 1.3 million generated conversations.

      
Before this I worked on a mental health companion app during my internship, built a tool that solves equations written in mid-air, and fine-tuned a small language model to correct Hindi grammar. I also enjoy the systems end of computing, most recently a virtual memory simulator written in C++.

      
I teach too. As a TA I run labs and tutorials for data structures, AI and NLP courses.
